Early source release. Build locally; no notarized installer yet.

Keep paused players handy

Qualifying loaded and paused players stay visible. Click a source’s name to open its exact tab or app.

Control each source

Play/pause, mute, and adjust volume for supported media. Browser tabs and profiles remain separate.

Keep sound and mic separate

Sound controls don’t change your microphone. Unsupported or unknown mic states stay disabled.

A small browser companion

The extension connects your browser to SoundShelf. Its popup shows connection status and your extension ID, with a Reconnect button.

The playback controls live in your Mac’s menu bar. The browser popup is just for the connection.

What works today

Browser media
Chrome is the initial target. Bridge registration also supports Brave, Edge, and Chromium; verify your browser. Safari and Firefox are not supported.
Native apps
Spotify and Music, through macOS Automation permission. Arbitrary native-app volume and output routing are outside the current scope.
Microphones
Conditional Google Meet controls for recognized English live-call UI. Discord and native call apps provide discovery only. This is not a universal microphone mute switch.
Privacy
No account, audio recording, or cloud service. The companion needs HTTP/HTTPS page access for media discovery and supported call controls. Metadata and commands stay in a local bridge.

Build and run

Declared requirements: macOS 13+, Swift 5.9+, and Python 3.8+ for browser integration. Not every OS/browser combination has been tested.

In Terminal
git clone https://github.com/RastogiAbhijeet/sound-shelf.git
cd sound-shelf
sh scripts/build-app.sh
open dist/SoundShelf.app

Look for SoundShelf’s wave-ring icon in the menu bar. To connect browser tabs, load the unpacked companion and register its extension ID using the setup guide.
